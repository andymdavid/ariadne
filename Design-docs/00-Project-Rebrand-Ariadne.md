# Project Rebrand: Ariadne

## New Project Identity

**Product Name:** Ariadne  
**Tagline:** "Your thread through content"  
**Mythological Reference:** Ariadne provided the thread that guided Theseus through the labyrinth

## Brand Positioning

Like Ariadne's thread that guided Theseus through the maze, our AI guides creators through the labyrinth of long-form content to find the golden clips worth sharing.

### Why Ariadne Works Perfectly

1. **Navigation & Guidance** - Core function of finding clips in complex content
2. **Thread Metaphor** - Perfect for timeline/editing workflows  
3. **Intelligence & Problem-Solving** - Exactly what our AI provides
4. **Sophisticated Sound** - Professional tool with mythological depth
5. **Memorable & Unique** - Distinctive in the content creation space

### Updated Branding Elements

**Product Vision:**
"Ariadne is the AI-powered content companion that transforms long-form podcasts into ready-to-publish social media reels. Just as Ariadne's thread guided heroes through the labyrinth, our intelligent system guides creators through hours of content to discover the moments worth sharing."

**Key Messaging:**
- "Your thread through content"
- "Navigate your content, craft your story"  
- "Find your way to great clips"
- "From labyrinth to library - discover your best moments"

**Voice & Tone:**
- Intelligent yet approachable
- Sophisticated but not intimidating  
- Helpful guide, not robotic assistant
- Mythological wisdom meets modern efficiency

### Logo Concepts

**Primary Symbol:** Stylized thread or path
**Secondary Elements:** Maze/labyrinth motifs, timeline elements
**Color Palette:** Sophisticated dark mode friendly colors

### Domain & Naming

**Potential Domains:**
- ariadne.ai
- getariadne.com  
- ariadne.app
- ariadnecreator.com

**Social Handles:**
- @AriadneApp
- @AriadneAI

This rebrand elevates the project from a generic "AI Reel Creator" to a sophisticated, memorable brand with deep meaning that perfectly captures our core value proposition.
